What is SDG 6?

In 2015 the United Nations agreed on 17 Sustainable Development Goals for 2030. Goal 6 is to make sure everyone has clean water and sanitation.

That means safe water to drink, a clean toilet, soap and water to wash hands, and using water carefully so rivers and groundwater do not run dry.
