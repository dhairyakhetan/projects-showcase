Concept 01A room fridge with three climates

Every drink, exactly right.

STRATA is a 45-litre fridge for your room with a real freezer and two drink zones. Sensors read the temperature of every can and bottle, so your phone can tell you which drink is ready to serve.

01The problem

One fridge, one temperature.

A normal fridge keeps everything near 4°C. That suits beer and soda, chills red wine past its best, and can't hold ice cream at all.

Typical serving ranges, °C. A single-temperature fridge serves all of them at the dashed line.

02Three zones

Three climates in 45 litres.

A compressor loop runs the freezer. Thermoelectric modules hold the two drink zones, and each zone is set on its own. Try it: the fridge on the right follows these controls.

03Sensing

It measures the drink, not the air.

Other fridges hold a zone's air at a set point. STRATA also looks at what's in it, so it knows which drink is ready and which is still cooling.

Infrared array

Reads the surface temperature of every can and bottle in the zone, calibrated against a probe.

Zone camera

Identifies each drink and counts them, so the right target is set without typing anything.

From public product listings and launch coverage, checked November 2026. “Not listed” means the source doesn't mention the feature. Prices are as listed, in local currency.

  1. Per-drink readings

    Coolers hold a zone's air at a set point. Samsung's AI Vision Inside recognises up to 33 fresh foods and can't see the freezer or door bins.4 None of these list a per-item temperature. STRATA reads each container.

  2. A freezer beside two drink zones

    The dual- and triple-zone coolers we found bottom out at 41°F and 34°F.2,3 Ice cream needs −18°C. STRATA puts both in one 45 L body.

  3. Priced for a room

    A 50 L mini fridge costs about ₹9,200; Samsung's AI fridge costs ₹4,00,690 in India.1,5 STRATA targets $349, closer to the first than the second.

  4. Stores, then fast-chills

    Rapid chillers like Juno and V-Tex cool one drink on demand but hold nothing.6,7 STRATA keeps a stock ready and can push one drink colder when you're in a hurry.

08Business model

The business on one page.

Hardware sold direct carries the margin. The app adds a small recurring line, and cafés and offices follow once the product is proven in rooms.
